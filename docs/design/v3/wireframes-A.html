Two courts, one night, end to end

Every frame is 390px wide, on the Organic system in its dark register: warm near-black ground, cream text, sage as the action colour, terracotta reserved for live and destructive moments. Caprasimo carries headings and every score. The night walked here is the sixteen-player one: eight a side, target three, six matches per court, two independent endings and no crossover final. Ties are settled by who reached the score first — no coin flip anywhere.

Job 2 · The middle two hours

Nothing set at 7:00 is a contract. People arrive, leave, swap courts, and scores get corrected — every change is a sheet away and never costs anyone else a game. Both courts run the same loop independently. Least-played-first has a property worth stating: on an eight-player court, played counts can never drift more than one game apart, so the four watching are effectively on next. The balance rule keeps the newest player from being hunted — if Chizea is on court, another C-tier is across the net.

Job 4 · Two endings, chosen per court

Court 2 runs a bracket, Court 1 plays one more round and crowns the top of its table. Seeding splits adjacent seeds — 1+3, 2+4, 5+7, 6+8 — so no pair holds both top seeds and any pair can beat any pair. There is deliberately no crossover final: the two champions are equally the story of the night.

26 Awkward headcounts

When the count is awkward

Guide

The app states the consequence rather than hiding the maths.

Twenty · ten a side

Target must be 4, because ten by three does not divide into fours. Ten matches per court. The playoff makes five pairs: seeds 1 to 6 pair normally, 9+10 form the fifth pair, and pairs four and five play one play-in for the last semi slot.

Eighteen · nine a side

Target 4 again. The ninth seed joins the fourth pair as a rotating trio, so nobody watches the climax.

Sixteen or twenty-four

Eight or twelve a side divides cleanly at either target. Nothing to explain.

States · Empty, error, confirm

Empty states invite an action. Errors say what happened and what to do, never apologise. Every destructive action names exactly what will be lost.
